# References and data-use notes

These are external references consulted during development. Review the current terms on each source before distributing any data or derivative dataset.

1. **NAOMi:** Song A, Gauthier JL, Pillow JW, Tank DW, Charles AS. “Neural anatomy and optical microscopy (NAOMi) simulation for evaluating calcium imaging methods.” *Journal of Neuroscience Methods* 358 (2021), 109173. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jneumeth.2021.109173 . Original paper describes a simulator for two-photon calcium fluorescence, not brightfield. Public upstream MATLAB implementation: https://bitbucket.org/adamshch/naomi_sim/ . A partial Julia port and its license/notice are at https://github.com/JuliaNeuroscience/NAOMi.jl . The code in this NAOBF prototype is newly written; it does not bundle NAOMi code.

2. **AxoNet / NeuroMorpho-derived morphology masks:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/broadinstitute/axonet-neuromorpho-dataset . Dataset card reports 7,158 traced neurons / ~164,000 views with soma/axon/dendrite semantic masks and CC BY 4.0 for the dataset. It also notes the underlying NeuroMorpho.org source data are subject to NeuroMorpho.org terms. Citation/attribution from its dataset card: Ascoli GA, Donohue DE, Halavi M (2007), “NeuroMorpho.Org: A Central Resource for Neuronal Morphologies,” *Journal of Neuroscience* 27:9247–9251. These traced morphologies provide shape priors, not cultured-brightfield image textures.

3. **CAFI / SH-SY5Y confocal brightfield reference:** https://zenodo.org/records/5596603 . The record describes 512×512 8-bit TIFF time series/z-stacks of SH-SY5Y cells using a Leica SP8 confocal brightfield setup at 63×, NA 1.40, with a 6.3 GB archive. The visible Zenodo record does not show a clear license in the metadata captured during development. Verify rights before reuse/redistribution; “confocal brightfield” is not necessarily equivalent to a conventional transmitted-brightfield bench setup.

4. **SH-SY5Y phase-contrast morphology and three-class workflow:** https://zenodo.org/records/19634700 and https://github.com/YordanovLab/PhaseContrastNeuriteAnalyzer . Dataset record describes phase-contrast SH-SY5Y images, camera/microscope metadata, example masks, and labels for background/cell body/neurite. It is specifically phase contrast, not ordinary brightfield. Check the data record's current license before reuse; do not assume the software release license automatically covers the separate dataset.

5. **LIVECell:** https://github.com/sartorius-research/LIVECell . Large manually annotated dataset of label-free live-cell phase-contrast images; includes SH-SY5Y. Images/annotations are under CC BY-NC 4.0; software under MIT, according to the official README. Useful for label-free segmentation practice where its non-commercial license fits, but not a brightfield ground-truth reference.

6. **Small actual hippocampal-neuron brightfield video reference:** Joy et al., “Synapses without tension fail to fire in an in vitro network of hippocampal neurons” (PNAS, 2023), article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10756289/ . Supplementary movie URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/10756289/bin/pnas.2311995120.sm01.mov . It shows neuronal culture in a specialized 3D force-sensor/ECM platform; use as a visual reference, not as a conventional dish/camera calibration target.

7. **Label-free, brightfield soma/neurite segmentation precedent:** VISTA-FISH paper, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12637628/ . Its reported workflow used separate Cellpose-SAM models for soma and neurite segmentation on brightfield tiles, with 50 manually annotated soma tiles and 6 neurite tiles. This is an example that the segmentation task itself has been attempted on brightfield images; inspect the original paper/supplement for the exact cell preparation and conditions before assuming domain compatibility.
